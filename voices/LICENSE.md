# Terms of use of the voice data (voices/ and docs/samples/)

The voice banks in this folder (`voices/inoue/`, `voices/mori/`) and the synthesized samples in `docs/` contain the
voices of real people (Inoue and Mori). They are provided only for the purposes below.

You may use them only to:

- try, develop and evaluate this pipeline and other speech research;
- generate synthetic dialogue data for **research** on spoken dialogue systems;
- show results in academic publications and presentations.

You may not:

- impersonate the speakers, or make them appear to say something in a way that could mislead others;
- use the voices in products, advertisements or any commercial service;
- redistribute the voice banks outside a copy or fork of this repository;
- use the voices in any way that could harm the speakers or their reputation.

Synthesized data made with these voices inherits these restrictions. For other uses, contact the repository owners.
The terms of the TTS model also apply (see THIRD_PARTY.md).
